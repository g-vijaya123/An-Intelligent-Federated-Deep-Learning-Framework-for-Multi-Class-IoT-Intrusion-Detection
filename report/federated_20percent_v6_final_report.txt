Scientific note:
The three source datasets have different original feature schemas and attack distributions. They were mapped into a shared 16-feature representation and 13-class taxonomy. Missing semantic features in a source dataset may be represented by zeros, so cross-domain distribution shift remains a limitation.

The aggregation used here gives equal influence to the three dataset clients. Therefore it should be described as equal-client federated averaging / FedAvg-style aggregation rather than canonical sample-count-weighted FedAvg.
